# Innovation-and-climate-change-in-US-Agriculture
## The project analyzes the link between crop innovation and climate change damages mitigation in US  agriculture.

This project is an extension based on the original paper of Moscona and Sastry, Quartely Journal of 
Economics, 2023 entitled "DOES DIRECTED INNOVATION MITIGATE CLIMATE DAMAGE? EVIDENCE FROM U.S. AGRICULTURE" [here](https://academic.oup.com/qje/article/138/2/637/6760169?guestAccessKey=)

## Research questions :
#### 1 - Effect of heat exposure on crop yields
#### 2 - Heat Exposure and introduction of new varieties
#### 3 - Local Adaptation and reselience 

## Available datasets : 
crop_level_data.dta: panel at the crop-decade level, with key variables

county_level_data.dta: panel at the county-decade level, with key variables

us_panel_short_burkeemmerick: panel at the county x year level from paper 
Adaptation to Climate Change: Evidence from US Agriculture (Burke and Emmerick, 
AEJ: Economic Policy, 2016)

crop_x_county_shocks.csv : county-level dataset with variables on county-level heat 
exposure for each crop each decade
